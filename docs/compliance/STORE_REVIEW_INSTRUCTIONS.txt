TAILMANCE - APP REVIEW NOTES (App Store Connect "Notes" / Google Play "App access")
=====================================================================================
Use only after the review account is prepared as described in the runbook. Paste the
text between the two lines into the store console. Do NOT add credentials
to this file or to the repository: enter the review account's e-mail and password only in
App Store Connect > App Review Information > Sign-In Information and in Google Play
Console > App content > App access (see docs/STORE_REVIEW_ACCESS_RUNBOOK.md).
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tailmance is a social app for pet owners (profiles, feed, Stories and Reels, matches,
chat, nearby discovery, Lost Paw alerts). Users must be 18 or older. Launch market:
Istanbul, Turkiye.

SIGN IN
- Use the e-mail and password entered in "Sign-In Information". It is an ordinary
  Tailmance account with the same production authentication as every user.
- There is no reviewer-only login, code or bypass. Two-step verification is optional and
  is not enrolled on this account, so no verification code is needed.
- The account is 18+ eligible, has accepted the current legal documents, has a complete
  pet profile and contains only synthetic, non-personal sample content.

WHERE TO FIND THE REQUIRED FEATURES
- Report / Block: open any pet profile > Report or Block. Blocked pets are listed in
  Settings > Blocked pets, where a block can be removed. Reports are reviewed by the
  moderation team; the user sees the status in the report history.
- Delete account: Settings > Permanently delete account (in-app, no e-mail needed). Active
  store subscriptions are managed in the App Store / Google Play subscription settings.
  The same request is available without the app at the "Delete account" page of our website.
- Data export: Settings > Export my data.
- Subscriptions: Premium screen. Purchases use Apple StoreKit sandbox / Google Play
  license testing. "Restore purchases" is on the same screen. Premium is granted only
  through the normal store entitlement; there is no review override.
- Lost Paw alerts: a pet owner can raise an alert for a lost pet and nearby owners are
  notified.
- Terms, Privacy Policy and Community Guidelines: Settings and the sign-up flow; also on
  our website.

PERMISSIONS (each is requested only when the related feature is used)
- Camera and microphone: pet photos, Stories, Reels, pet verification and matched-pet
  audio/video calls.
- Photos: only the photos and videos the user picks.
- Location (while in use): approximate location, to show nearby pets by distance.
- Notifications: matches, messages and Lost Paw alerts; optional marketing messages
  need separate consent.

VACCINATION BOOKLET CHECK
- Owners can upload the pages of a pet's vaccination booklet. An AI-assisted document
  check compares dated vaccinations and parasite treatments with the schedule for the
  pet's species and age; unclear pages are passed to a person. The result is shown as
  "checked and approved" or not approved. It is not a statement about the pet's health
  and is not a veterinary examination. The screen states "AI can make mistakes, please
  check." There are no veterinarian, clinic or registry features.

MATING
- A "Mating" discovery purpose exists only as a filter between pet owners. Tailmance takes
  no part in any mating and gives no guarantee about it; owners arrange everything among
  themselves. The in-app filter text says the same.

CONTENT SAFETY
- User content is moderated; users can report content, block pets and appeal decisions.
  Conversations are between pets that matched with each other.

Contact for the review team: support address shown on the website's Support page.
