## GAP-004 — Standard daily quota / Premium entitlement contract

Status: **CLOSED SOURCE, REVALIDATED**.

The server-authoritative contract remains:

- Standard: 50 discovery swipes per local-day quota window by default.
- Standard: 3 newly initiated conversations per local-day quota window by default.
- Operator-configurable limits are bounded and become effective only on the next quota window; active-window limits and timezone are frozen until reset.
- Premium: no product quota; abuse/security rate limits remain independent.
- A Premium first message permanently unlocks that conversation for the Standard participant.
- Replies in an already initiated conversation do not consume another new-conversation slot.
- The historical two-free-message model is not authoritative.

No new GAP-004 source mutation was required in RC-199. The existing migrations 196–197 and client projection were revalidated.

## GAP-006 — Health-document terminology

Status: **CLOSED SOURCE, HARDENED**.

RC-199 removes the remaining semantic ambiguity in the approved health-verification state. `verification.healthApproved` now explicitly names health/veterinary **document review** in all 19 runtime locales instead of using a generic veterinary-verification signal.

The regression contract now requires locale-appropriate document terminology not only in the verification subtitle, hero and health title, but also in the approved-state copy. Existing safeguards continue to reject misleading healthy/disease-free/verified-health claims.

## GAP-007 — Tailmance Premium naming

Status: **CLOSED SOURCE, REVALIDATED**.

All runtime and store product copy continues to use **Tailmance Premium / Premium**. Historical `Plus` references are not allowed in live product branding or product language. Incidental non-brand uses such as an icon named `plus` or the French word `plus` are not product-brand references and remain valid.

No new GAP-007 source mutation was required in RC-199.
