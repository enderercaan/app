# Tailmance — Product Strategy, Retention & Monetization Boundary — 2026-09-30

## Core promise
Tailmance is the digital dog park: help a dog find compatible nearby playmates, then give owners safe reasons to return every week through local feed, walks/meetups, lost-pet alerts, pet-friendly places and community events.

## Density before geography
- Dog discovery only; cats are feed-only.
- Istanbul pilot neighborhoods: Moda, Fenerbahçe, Caddebostan.
- Open-neighborhood target: 300 active dogs; 300–500 is the desired density band.
- The 40-paw referral milestone is a growth signal, not an automatic unlock. Operator approval remains required so a thin discovery pool cannot silently open.

## Retention loops
- Premium host / free join community events, including walks, playdates and weekly recurrence.
- Free proximity-scoped Lost Paw alerts.
- Pet-friendly places and in-app public-place meetup selection.
- Local pet-only feed is the default; native social sharing carries Tailmance branding and pet media without auto-posting on the user's behalf.
- Safety and Lost Paw remain free.

## Trust and account integrity
- Visible pet/owner verification indicators.
- Phone verification plus Apple/Google identity linking.
- 18+ and legal gates remain authoritative.
- Pet-first enforcement rejects disguised human-dating use.

## Messaging economics
- Standard self-initiated messaging retains its quota.
- If Premium initiates a conversation, the Standard recipient gets a courtesy reply path so the Premium user's value is not destroyed by the recipient's tier.

## Premium
Premium includes Likes You, unlimited product likes subject only to anti-abuse ceilings, advanced filters, Travel Mode, multi-pet, Incognito, event hosting and recurring Boost value. Boost and Super Like can also be sold as consumables.

## Mating boundary
Tailmance does not sell animals, broker breeding, set stud fees, arrange mating, guarantee suitability, handle payment or participate in owners' private mating decision. Where jurisdiction/welfare gates permit the feature, Tailmance may only surface owner-arranged dog profiles that satisfy the product's basic health/welfare signals. Veterinary fitness and the owners' final decision remain outside Tailmance. Commercial breeding, animal sales and stud-fee advertising remain prohibited.

## Business monetization
- Monthly operator-managed plans for pet-friendly places, veterinarians, groomers and trainers.
- Featured placement only while entitlement is active and always disclosed.
- Sponsorship/sample/event offers are disclosed.
- External event-ticket links support referral attribution. Confirmed partner/provider conversion evidence is recorded service-role-only and calculates the configured commission deterministically and idempotently.
- No user-data sales, pet sales or aggressive advertising.
- Insurance affiliate, commerce affiliate and sitter marketplace remain future fail-closed features.

## Deliberate exclusions
- No AI veterinary diagnosis feature is enabled. Health tracking and verified veterinary signals exist, but diagnosis/advice would require a separate clinical safety, regulatory and provider-quality program.
- No adoption marketplace is enabled in this product scope.
