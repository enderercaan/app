Jurisdiction-Specific Privacy Layer

This document explains how Tailmance applies country-specific privacy information. It is not a consent form and does not replace the exact Country-Specific Legal Supplement for your verified legal country.

1. Exact legal context

Tailmance determines country-specific obligations from the verified legal country and, where required, the applicable subnational context. Changing the app language, travelling, or changing a store region does not by itself change that legal context.

2. Data and purposes

The categories of personal data and processing purposes described in the Privacy Notice remain applicable. Additional or stricter local requirements are applied through the exact country-specific supplement and runtime legal controls.

3. Rights and requests

Available rights, request procedures, response periods and regulator routes vary by applicable law. The exact country-specific supplement describes the local layer. In-app data export and account/data deletion routes remain available where applicable.

4. Transfers and retention

International-transfer safeguards, local storage or localization duties, and retention requirements are applied according to the verified legal context. Tailmance does not represent one universal retention period or transfer mechanism as valid for every country.

5. Marketing and consent

Optional marketing choices are handled separately from this notice. Receiving or acknowledging this document does not create consent where applicable law requires a separate affirmative choice.

6. Country-specific document

The Legal Center presents the exact country-specific supplement for your legal country. If an accessibility translation is shown, it does not replace the required or binding-language version identified by Tailmance.

7. Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to yektaozel@icloud.com.
