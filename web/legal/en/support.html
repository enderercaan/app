Animal health

Tailmance is not a veterinary or emergency health service. For urgent animal-health concerns, contact an appropriate veterinary service directly.

AI can make mistakes. AI-assisted document checks are preliminary only: they never make a final or legally valid decision about an animal’s health, are not veterinary advice, and do not guarantee health or vaccination status. Always consult a licensed veterinarian.
