Subscription and Consumer Information

This page explains Tailmance Premium and consumer information that applies before and after purchase. Mandatory local consumer rights always remain available.

1. Premium

Premium is optional. Standard use continues without a subscription. Premium features are described in the app and may change prospectively subject to applicable consumer law.

2. Price and billing

The binding price, currency, taxes, billing period, trial, and promotion terms are the values shown by the App Store or Google Play immediately before purchase. Tailmance does not directly collect your payment-card number.

3. Automatic renewal

Unless the store clearly states otherwise, Premium renews automatically until cancelled. Renewal timing and charges follow the relevant store terms and applicable law.

4. Cancellation

Manage or cancel through the store account used for purchase. Deleting your Tailmance account does not automatically cancel the store subscription. Cancellation generally stops future renewals and does not itself create a refund right.

5. Refunds, withdrawal and mandatory rights

Refund, cooling-off, withdrawal, statutory conformity, and digital-service rights depend on the store and applicable consumer law. Nothing on this page limits rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

6. Account deletion and entitlement

If you delete the Tailmance account while a store subscription remains active, store billing may continue until separately cancelled. Restoring or transferring entitlement is subject to the store account and Tailmance's verified subscription records.

7. Purchase presentation

Before an in-app purchase, Tailmance records the exact consumer document set presented to you. If the legal bundle or document hash changes before the store sheet opens, the purchase flow must stop and require a fresh review.

8. Local information

Country- or region-specific operator details, tax information, withdrawal instructions, dispute-resolution routes, or other mandatory disclosures are provided in the applicable local supplement when required.
