Moderation Decisions and Appeals

Tailmance uses moderation to protect users, animals, the service, and legal compliance. Eligible decisions include an explanation and an appeal route where appropriate.

1. Decisions

Tailmance may remove, limit, demote, or restrict content, features, or accounts for Community Guidelines, Terms, safety, fraud, animal-welfare, or legal reasons. Decisions are recorded with a user-visible reason unless disclosure is prohibited for safety or legal reasons.

2. Appeals

Where an appeal is available, submit it through the app or stated route within the applicable period. Appeals are reviewed with the decision evidence and may involve human review. A successful appeal does not override a separate valid restriction.

3. Restoration

A reversed decision is not treated as complete until the affected product state is actually restored where restoration is appropriate. Conflicting active decisions prevent unsafe automatic restoration.

4. Account restrictions

If account access is restricted, Tailmance provides an appropriate reason and available appeal route unless disclosure is legally or safety restricted. Support and required legal/privacy routes remain available where they must remain accessible.

5. External rights

The internal appeal process does not remove any right you may have to contact a regulator, court, dispute-resolution body, or other competent authority under applicable law.
