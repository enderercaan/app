Accessibility

Tailmance treats accessible use as a core product requirement and aims to support a broad range of users and assistive technologies.

Accessible use

The interface is designed with clear labels, readable text, contrast, scalable text, suitable touch targets, and assistive-technology compatibility. Any higher mandatory accessibility right under applicable law remains unaffected.

Assistive technologies

If you experience an issue with VoiceOver, TalkBack, larger text, switch access, keyboard navigation, or another accessibility feature, contact us and, where useful, include device, operating-system, and assistive-technology details.

Accessibility request

If you cannot use a screen, control, or item of content, or need a reasonable accommodation, contact yektaozel@icloud.com. Do not send personal or sensitive information that is unnecessary to explain the issue.

Local rights and scope

This page is not an independent accessibility certification or a blanket claim of compliance with every technical or legal standard. It does not limit non-waivable accessibility or consumer rights, and additional local rights may apply.
