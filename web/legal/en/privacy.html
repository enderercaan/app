Privacy Policy

Version 2026-09-29. Data controller: Yekta Özel. This Policy explains how Tailmance processes personal data. Country- or region-specific privacy supplements shown to you apply together with it. This Policy is not marketing consent.

1. Categories of data we process

We may process account and session data, including your email address and sign-in identifiers; security, device, network, IP and push-token data; crash and performance diagnostics; pet profiles, photos, posts, stories and private messages between matched pets; approximate discovery location; health, vaccination and verification records you choose to upload; owner identity-verification and age-assurance results where these features are used; subscription status and store transaction references; reports, blocks, moderation and appeals; notification and marketing preferences; and records proving which legal context and documents were shown to you.

2. Sources of data

We obtain data directly from you, your device and app use, content you upload, safety and moderation processes, the App Store or Google Play, authorized service providers, and in limited cases other users' reports or content.

3. Purposes

We process data to provide accounts and social features, discovery and matching, messaging and notifications, security and fraud prevention, moderation and appeals, subscription verification, user requests, data export and deletion, service reliability, and compliance with applicable law.

4. Legal bases and local differences

Depending on the purpose, data category, and applicable law, processing may rely on contract, legal obligation, legitimate interests, consent, or another lawful basis. Local privacy supplements explain jurisdiction-specific differences.

5. Notice is separate from marketing consent

A privacy notice informs you about processing; it is not blanket consent. Marketing push notifications require a separate, optional, withdrawable choice and do not authorize email, SMS, phone, or other marketing channels unless separately permitted.

6. Location

If you allow location access, Tailmance may use device location for discovery. Coordinates are rounded to three decimal places before being sent to the server, stored privately, and are not shown to other users; only approximate distance is displayed. Device and store location signals do not by themselves determine legal jurisdiction.

7. Health and sensitive data

Pet health and vaccination records may contain sensitive information. Upload only what is necessary and avoid third-party data. Where identity, facial, biometric, or other sensitive information is used for age assurance, additional notice, safeguards, retention limits, provider information, and alternative procedures must be supplied as required.

AI-assisted health document check. Where you upload the pages of your pet's vaccination booklet or other health record to Tailmance, Tailmance may process them with AI-assisted text recognition. You are asked to upload every filled page so that the record can be assessed as a whole. The check compares the dated entries of the document, within the required date ranges, with the vaccinations and the internal and external parasite treatments that apply according to the information you entered in the pet profile (species and date of birth). Other care entries and the breed you entered are not checked. Tailmance does not determine a pet's breed from images and does not examine or diagnose any animal. Where the record is complete, consistent and current, it is marked as “checked and approved” until the first date on which a vaccination or treatment falls due again. Records that are incomplete, unclear, inconsistent or outdated are not approved automatically and may be reviewed by a person. You may supplement the record at any time by uploading further pages. The label “checked and approved” records only that the vaccinations and internal and external parasite treatments shown in the document were found to have been completed within the required date ranges. It is not a statement that the animal is in good health, is not veterinary advice, diagnosis or certification, is not a guarantee of any kind and is not a final, binding or legally valid decision. No veterinarian, clinic or register takes part in or confirms the check. The pages are processed on Tailmance's behalf by a cloud text-recognition provider under a data processing agreement, in that provider's European Union region, and are not used by the provider to train its models. AI can make mistakes and may overlook errors or misread a document, so you are asked to check important information yourself. This notice applies to all information and guidance provided by AI-assisted features. Consult a licensed veterinarian for any health decision and do not delay care because of a result shown in the app.

8. Sharing and processors

Data may be shared with authorized infrastructure, security, moderation, messaging, notification, analytics/crash, subscription, and other providers only as needed for the service, and with public authorities where lawfully required. Tailmance does not sell personal data to advertisers.

9. International transfers

10. Retention

Data is retained only for as long as necessary for the purpose or required by law. Different categories have different periods. Account deletion does not require immediate deletion of limited security, fraud, dispute, accounting, or lawful-request records that must be retained.

11. Security

Tailmance uses technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect data, including access control, encryption in transit, private storage controls, audit records, and abuse prevention. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Local supplements explain the exact rights and procedures.

13. Children and age

Tailmance accounts are for adults aged 18 or older. This product rule does not replace age-assurance, child-safety, reporting, or platform duties that may apply under law or store policies. If Tailmance learns that an ineligible minor created an account, appropriate restriction and deletion steps may be taken.

14. Automated systems and moderation

Automated systems may assist safety, fraud detection, ranking, recommendations, and moderation. Where applicable law requires explanations, human review, objection, or other safeguards for consequential decisions, those rights will be provided.

AI-assisted features. Some features use AI-assisted tools. The pages of vaccination booklets are read together with AI-assisted text recognition and compared, within the required date ranges, with the vaccinations and internal and external parasite treatments that apply to the pet according to its species and date of birth; a record that is complete and current is marked as “checked and approved”, and any other record is not approved automatically and may be reviewed by a person. The check does not determine breed from images and does not diagnose, and the mark is not a statement about the animal's health. AI can make mistakes, so please check important information. AI output is only a signal: it is not veterinary advice or a diagnosis and does not by itself decide a consequential outcome for a user. Tailmance does not offer an AI chatbot or AI veterinary diagnosis. A user affected by an AI-assisted result can ask for human review through the in-app appeal process or Support.

15. Changes

Material changes to this Policy or a local supplement will be communicated where required. If a change requires renewed consent or acknowledgement, Tailmance will request it rather than relying on an old choice.

16. Contact

Use the Support and Contact page for privacy requests. Where a local representative, privacy contact, data-protection authority, or other jurisdiction-specific contact is legally required, the relevant local supplement will provide it.
