Privacy Policy

Version 2026-09-29. Data controller: Yekta Özel. This Policy explains how Tailmance processes personal data. Country- or region-specific privacy supplements shown to you apply together with it. This Policy is not marketing consent.

1. Categories of data we process

We may process account and session data; security, device, network, IP and push-token data; pet profiles, photos and social interactions; approximate discovery location; health, vaccination and verification records you choose to upload; subscription status and store transaction references; reports, blocks, moderation and appeals; notification and marketing preferences; and records proving which legal context and documents were shown to you.

2. Sources of data

We obtain data directly from you, your device and app use, content you upload, safety and moderation processes, the App Store or Google Play, authorized service providers, and in limited cases other users' reports or content.

3. Purposes

We process data to provide accounts and social features, discovery and matching, messaging and notifications, security and fraud prevention, moderation and appeals, subscription verification, user requests, data export and deletion, service reliability, and compliance with applicable law.

4. Legal bases and local differences

Depending on the purpose, data category, and applicable law, processing may rely on contract, legal obligation, legitimate interests, consent, or another lawful basis. Local privacy supplements explain jurisdiction-specific differences.

5. Notice is separate from marketing consent

A privacy notice informs you about processing; it is not blanket consent. Marketing push notifications require a separate, optional, withdrawable choice and do not authorize email, SMS, phone, or other marketing channels unless separately permitted.

6. Location

If you allow location access, Tailmance may use device location for discovery. Coordinates are rounded to three decimal places before being sent to the server, stored privately, and are not shown to other users; only approximate distance is displayed. Device and store location signals do not by themselves determine legal jurisdiction.

7. Health and sensitive data

Pet health and vaccination records may contain sensitive information. Upload only what is necessary and avoid third-party data. Where identity, facial, biometric, or other sensitive information is used for age assurance, additional notice, safeguards, retention limits, provider information, and alternative procedures must be supplied as required.

8. Sharing and processors

Data may be shared with authorized infrastructure, security, moderation, messaging, notification, analytics/crash, subscription, and other providers only as needed for the service, and with public authorities where lawfully required. Tailmance does not sell personal data to advertisers.

9. International transfers

Where data is transferred internationally, Tailmance must use the mechanism and safeguards required by the applicable jurisdiction and provide any required local notice. Transfer rules may differ by country or region.

10. Retention

Data is retained only for as long as necessary for the purpose or required by law. Different categories have different periods. Account deletion does not require immediate deletion of limited security, fraud, dispute, accounting, or lawful-request records that must be retained.

11. Security

Tailmance uses technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect data, including access control, encryption in transit, private storage controls, audit records, and abuse prevention. No system can guarantee absolute security.

12. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, portability, withdraw consent, challenge certain automated decisions, complain to a regulator, and obtain information about recipients and transfers. Local supplements explain the exact rights and procedures.

13. Children and age

Tailmance accounts are for adults aged 18 or older. This product rule does not replace age-assurance, child-safety, reporting, or platform duties that may apply under law or store policies. If Tailmance learns that an ineligible minor created an account, appropriate restriction and deletion steps may be taken.

14. Automated systems and moderation

Automated systems may assist safety, fraud detection, ranking, recommendations, and moderation. Where applicable law requires explanations, human review, objection, or other safeguards for consequential decisions, those rights will be provided.

AI-assisted features. Some features use AI-assisted tools. On supported devices, an on-device model can suggest a breed from a pet photo and screen photos for safety before upload; suggestions are optional and may be wrong. Vaccination and veterinary documents may be pre-checked with AI-assisted text recognition, and unclear results go to human review. AI output is only a signal: it is not veterinary advice or a diagnosis and does not by itself decide a consequential outcome for a user. Tailmance does not offer an AI chatbot or AI veterinary diagnosis. A user affected by an AI-assisted result can ask for human review through the in-app appeal process or Support.

15. Changes

Material changes to this Policy or a local supplement will be communicated where required. If a change requires renewed consent or acknowledgement, Tailmance will request it rather than relying on an old choice.

16. Contact

Use the Support and Contact page for privacy requests. Where a local representative, privacy contact, data-protection authority, or other jurisdiction-specific contact is legally required, the relevant local supplement will provide it.
