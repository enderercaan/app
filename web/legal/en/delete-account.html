Delete Your Account and Data

The fastest way to delete a Tailmance account is Settings → Permanently delete account. If you cannot access the app, you may submit a deletion request on the web without reinstalling it.

Web deletion request

Requests are processed only after appropriate account-owner verification. The web form is designed not to disclose to third parties whether an email address is associated with a Tailmance account.

Enter only the email address associated with your account. This form does not reveal the existence of an account to third parties.

What is deleted and what may be retained

Deletion covers the account, pet profiles, owned social content and photos, account-linked messaging/match records, private health/vaccination records, private location, and preferences, subject to applicable retention rules. Limited security, fraud-prevention, dispute, accounting, or legal records may be retained where law requires or permits it, as explained in the Privacy Policy and local supplements.
