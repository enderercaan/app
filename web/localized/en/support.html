Animal health

Tailmance is not a veterinary or emergency health service. For urgent animal-health concerns, contact an appropriate veterinary service directly.

AI can make mistakes; please check. Upload every filled page of the vaccination booklet. The AI-assisted check only compares the dated entries, within the required date ranges, with the vaccinations and the internal and external parasite treatments that apply to the pet, and may mark the booklet as “checked and approved”. This does not mean that the pet is in perfect health and is not veterinary advice, certification or a guarantee. This notice applies to all information and guidance given by AI.
