Intellectual Property and Infringement Notices

Rights holders and authorized representatives may report content on Tailmance that they believe infringes intellectual-property rights.

Submitting a notice

Send enough information to identify the protected work or right, locate the reported content, contact you, and show your authority to act. Include a good-faith statement and signature where applicable law requires it.

Review and counter-notice

Tailmance reviews notices under applicable law and safeguards against fraudulent or abusive claims. When content is restricted, the affected user may receive a reason and an available appeal or counter-notice process where appropriate.

Repeat infringement

Verified repeated infringement may lead to proportionate content or account restrictions. The number of notices alone does not establish infringement; mistaken and abusive notices are also considered.

United States 17 U.S.C. §512

Where the §512 procedure applies, the U.S.-specific supplement will identify the designated agent and required notice process. This general page does not claim that Tailmance has completed any registration or qualifies for a statutory safe harbor unless the U.S. supplement expressly confirms it.

Emergency or illegal content

An intellectual-property notice is not a substitute for emergency or illegal-content reporting. Use in-app reporting, the Safety Center, or Support for those concerns.
