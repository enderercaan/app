Community Guidelines

These rules apply together with the Terms to keep Tailmance respectful, safe, and focused on animal welfare. Stricter applicable local law takes priority.

1. Respect people and animals

Do not abuse or endanger animals, harass, threaten, stalk, exploit, discriminate against, or direct hate at others. Do not encourage unsafe animal handling or conceal material welfare risks.

2. Sexual exploitation and child safety

Child sexual abuse material, sexual exploitation of minors, grooming, solicitation, or content that facilitates such conduct is prohibited. Tailmance may preserve, restrict, and report material where required by law and provides a child-safety contact route.

3. Authentic profiles and health claims

Do not impersonate people, misrepresent pet ownership, breed, age, sex, health, vaccination, or verification status, or submit fraudulent documents. Verification badges are not veterinary guarantees.

AI can make mistakes; please check. Upload every filled page of the vaccination booklet. The AI-assisted check only compares the pages, within the required date ranges, with the vaccinations, internal and external parasite treatments and care that apply to the pet, and may mark the booklet as “checked and approved”. This does not mean that the pet is in perfect health and is not veterinary advice, certification or a guarantee. This notice applies to all information and guidance given by AI. Consult a licensed veterinarian.

4. Privacy and intimate content

Do not share another person's private data, precise location, credentials, medical information, or intimate imagery without authorization. Avoid placing unnecessary third-party personal data in pet records or messages.

5. Illegal goods, fraud and dangerous conduct

Do not use Tailmance for fraud, unlawful goods or services, weapons or dangerous activity, unauthorized financial schemes, or conduct that facilitates crime or evasion of lawful safety controls.

6. Pet sales and commercial breeding

Tailmance is not a pet-sales or breeder marketplace. Price negotiation, deposits, sale or delivery of animals, commercial breeder lead generation, unlawful breeding, trafficking, or puppy-mill activity are prohibited. The service also may not be used to offer, request, broker, or arrange adoption, rehoming, or transfer of ownership of a pet.

7. Mating and animal welfare

Mating features are disabled by default and may be enabled only after country-specific legal, welfare, age, health, and kinship controls are approved. Users must not use the service to promote harmful or unlawful breeding.

8. Reporting, blocking and moderation

Use in-app reporting and blocking for safety concerns. Tailmance may remove or limit content or accounts and will provide reasons and appeal routes where appropriate and legally required.

9. Local rules

Country- and region-specific illegal-content, child-safety, animal-welfare, reporting, preservation, and law-enforcement duties may add to these Guidelines. Tailmance applies the stricter applicable rule where required.
