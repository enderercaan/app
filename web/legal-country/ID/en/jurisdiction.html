Indonesia

Legal scope

This supplement applies when Tailmance determines that Indonesia is the country whose law applies to your account. It supplements the Terms, Privacy Notice, Community Guidelines, subscription notice and other legal documents; it does not replace them.

Tailmance applies legal obligations according to that verified country and, where required, the applicable regional or local legal context. Changing the app language, travelling, or changing a store region does not by itself change that legal context.

Document language

The binding legal documents for this legal configuration are maintained in the required language(s): Indonesian. When a specific legal-document language is required, Tailmance does not automatically substitute another language without notice.

This version is provided as an accessibility translation. It does not replace a required-language version where applicable law or this legal configuration requires one.

Privacy, safety and user rights

Privacy rights, data requests, account and data deletion, international transfers, age assurance, electronic marketing, security and breach duties are applied through the exact legal context. Use the in-app Legal Center for notices, export and deletion routes.

AI-assisted health document check. Where you upload the pages of your pet's vaccination booklet or other health record to Tailmance, Tailmance may process them with AI-assisted text recognition. You are asked to upload every filled page so that the record can be assessed as a whole. The check compares the dated entries of the document, within the required date ranges, with the vaccinations and the internal and external parasite treatments that apply according to the information you entered in the pet profile (species and date of birth). Other care entries and the breed you entered are not checked. Tailmance does not determine a pet's breed from images and does not examine or diagnose any animal. Where the record is complete, consistent and current, it is marked as “checked and approved” until the first date on which a vaccination or treatment falls due again. Records that are incomplete, unclear, inconsistent or outdated are not approved automatically and may be reviewed by a person. You may supplement the record at any time by uploading further pages. The label “checked and approved” records only that the vaccinations and internal and external parasite treatments shown in the document were found to have been completed within the required date ranges. It is not a statement that the animal is in good health, is not veterinary advice, diagnosis or certification, is not a guarantee of any kind and is not a final, binding or legally valid decision. No veterinarian, clinic or register takes part in or confirms the check. The pages are processed on Tailmance's behalf by a cloud text-recognition provider under a data processing agreement, in that provider's European Union region, and are not used by the provider to train its models. AI can make mistakes and may overlook errors or misread a document, so you are asked to check important information yourself. This notice applies to all information and guidance provided by AI-assisted features. Consult a licensed veterinarian for any health decision and do not delay care because of a result shown in the app.

Subscriptions and consumer information

The app store through which the purchase is made is authoritative for the displayed subscription price, currency and applicable tax treatment. Renewal, cancellation and required pre-contract information are presented before purchase. Tailmance does not sell pets or physical products and does not process peer-to-peer payments for animal sales.

Animal welfare and non-commerce

Commercial animal sales and commercial-breeding marketplace activity are prohibited. Owner-arranged mating is available only when the applicable country-specific animal-welfare, breeding, health, kinship and eligibility requirements are met; otherwise the feature remains unavailable. Owners who arrange a mating manage the whole process among themselves; Tailmance takes no part in it, gives no guarantee about it, its outcome or the health, fitness or pedigree of any animal and, to the extent permitted by law, is not responsible for arrangements between users. The AI-assisted document check is not such a guarantee, and AI can make mistakes, so please check.
