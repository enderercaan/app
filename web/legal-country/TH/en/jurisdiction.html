Jurisdiction-Specific Legal Supplement

Thailand

Legal scope

This supplement applies when Tailmance determines that Thailand is the country whose law applies to your account. It supplements the Terms, Privacy Notice, Community Guidelines, subscription notice and other legal documents; it does not replace them.

Tailmance applies legal obligations according to that verified country and, where required, the applicable regional or local legal context. Changing the app language, travelling, or changing a store region does not by itself change that legal context.

Document language

The binding legal documents for this legal configuration are maintained in the required language(s): Thai. When a specific legal-document language is required, Tailmance does not automatically substitute another language without notice.

This version is provided as an accessibility translation. It does not replace a required-language version where applicable law or this legal configuration requires one.

Country-specific legal requirements

Twenty-two areas of legal requirements are reviewed against attributable official sources for the applicable country. Current law and effective dates are separated from proposals, consultations and future measures; a proposal is never treated as current law.

Source review date: 1 October 2026. Future, draft or consultation-stage measures are tracked separately and are not represented as current law.

Privacy, safety and user rights

Privacy rights, data requests, account and data deletion, international transfers, age assurance, electronic marketing, security and breach duties are applied through the exact legal context. Use the in-app Legal Center for notices, export and deletion routes.

AI-assisted health document check. Where you upload the pages of your pet's vaccination booklet or other health record to Tailmance, Tailmance may process them with AI-assisted text recognition. You are asked to upload every filled page so that the record can be assessed as a whole. The check compares the content of the document, within the required date ranges, with the vaccinations, internal and external parasite treatments and routine care that apply according to the information you entered in the pet profile (species, age and the breed as entered by you). Tailmance does not determine a pet's breed from images and does not examine or diagnose any animal. Where the record is complete, consistent and current, it is marked as “checked and approved”. Records that are incomplete, unclear, inconsistent or outdated are not approved automatically and may be reviewed by a person. You may supplement the record at any time by uploading further pages. The label “checked and approved” records only that the vaccinations, internal and external parasite treatments and required care shown in the document were found to have been completed within the required date ranges. It is not a statement that the animal is in good health, is not veterinary advice, diagnosis or certification, is not a guarantee of any kind and is not a final, binding or legally valid decision. AI can make mistakes and may overlook errors or misread a document, so you are asked to check important information yourself. This notice applies to all information and guidance provided by AI-assisted features. Consult a licensed veterinarian for any health decision and do not delay care because of a result shown in the app.

Subscriptions and consumer information

The app store through which the purchase is made is authoritative for the displayed subscription price, currency and applicable tax treatment. Renewal, cancellation and required pre-contract information are presented before purchase. Tailmance does not sell pets or physical products and does not process peer-to-peer payments for animal sales.

Animal welfare and non-commerce

Commercial animal sales and commercial-breeding marketplace activity are prohibited. Owner-arranged mating is available only when the applicable country-specific animal-welfare, breeding, health, kinship and eligibility requirements are met; otherwise the feature remains unavailable.
